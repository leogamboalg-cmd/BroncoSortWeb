# BroncoSort Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 12, 2026

BroncoSort is a browser extension and website designed to help students compare and sort course sections, view professor-rating information, request support for additional schools, and access optional Premium features. This Privacy Policy explains what information BroncoSort handles, why it is used, and the choices available to users.

## Information BroncoSort Handles

BroncoSort may handle the following limited information when needed to provide its features:

- Professor names and course-section information displayed on supported school registration pages.
- Extension settings and preferences saved through Chrome extension storage.
- School-support requests and limited registration-page structure information submitted when a user requests compatibility for an unsupported school.
- If a user signs in for Premium, Google account information made available through Google Sign-In, such as the user's name, email address, profile picture, and account identifier.
- Premium account status, purchase status, and expiration information.
- Technical information required to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service, such as authentication tokens and server request or error information.

BroncoSort does not request or collect school login credentials, Google passwords, transcripts, private messages, or payment-card details. Payment-card information is entered directly into Stripe Checkout and is handled by Stripe, not BroncoSort.

## How Information Is Used

BroncoSort uses information only to:

- Display professor ratings and summaries.
- Sort and enhance course sections on supported registration pages.
- Save user settings and maintain supported extension functionality.
- Evaluate and add compatibility for requested schools.
- Authenticate users and associate Premium access with the correct account.
- Create and confirm Stripe Checkout sessions and manage Premium access.
- Protect, maintain, and troubleshoot BroncoSort.

BroncoSort does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

## Browser Access

The extension accesses relevant page content only on supported school pages where its features operate. It is not designed to collect browsing history or page content outside those supported pages. The extension uses Chrome extension storage to retain settings and information required for its functionality.

## Third-Party Services

BroncoSort relies on third-party services to provide certain features:

- **Google Sign-In** verifies the identity of Premium users. Google handles the user's password; BroncoSort receives a signed credential and permitted profile information.
- **Stripe** processes Premium payments. Stripe's handling of payment information is governed by Stripe's own privacy policy.
- **Rate My Professors** is a source of publicly available professor-rating information. BroncoSort is not affiliated with Rate My Professors.
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers** may process limited account and technical information as needed to operate the website and backend services.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## Information Sharing

BroncoSort does not sell or rent user information. Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to deliver the functions described above, comply with applicable law, protect users or the service, or respond to a valid legal request.

## Data Retention and Security

BroncoSort retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its features, maintain Premium access, meet legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. Locally stored extension information remains in the user's browser until it is cleared or the extension is removed, subject to Chrome's behavior.

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect information. However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

## User Choices

Users may:

- Use non-account features without signing in, where available.
- Sign out of Premium on the website.
- Clear locally stored website or extension data through browser settings.
- Remove the extension to stop its access to supported pages.
- Contact the developer to ask about account information or request deletion, subject to legal or operational retention requirements.

## Children's Privacy

BroncoSort is intended for students using college or university registration systems and is not directed to children under 13. BroncoSort does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when BroncoSort's features or data practices change. The revised policy will be posted with a new effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact the BroncoSort developer through the support contact provided on the [BroncoSort Chrome Web Store listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/broncosort/iimoegnkdjicpamkelchhfcibglblmnh).
